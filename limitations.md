# Limitations and Falsification

ORCA is an experimental coordination framework.

## Limitations

- Shared-state ground truth may be unavailable.
- Some conflicts are productive rather than harmful.
- Human roles may be intentionally ambiguous.
- Synchronization delay can be acceptable in asynchronous systems.
- Coordination metrics can reward bureaucracy rather than performance.
- Actor identifiers may not map cleanly to real organizational responsibility.

## Falsification criteria

ORCA's central hypothesis is weakened if:

1. Conventional workflow metrics predict failures equally well.
2. Handoff integrity does not correlate with task success.
3. Shared-state consistency adds no value.
4. Coordination-history recursion adds no value.
5. Results fail on held-out team structures.
6. High ORCA scores do not predict better collective outcomes.

## Claim boundary

ORCA measures coordination under an explicit operational model. It does not infer intent, trustworthiness, or consciousness.
